Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How quickly can I get started?

Most shops are up and running within 24 hours. Our onboarding team will help you set up your calendar integration and customize the AI for your shop.

What if I need help after setup?

We offer 24/7 customer support. Email us anytime at support@shopbookerai.com and we'll respond within 24 hours (usually much faster).

Can I customize what the AI says?

Absolutely! During setup, we'll train the AI on your specific services, pricing, hours, and brand voice. You can update these anytime.

What calendar systems do you integrate with?

We integrate with Google Calendar, Microsoft Outlook, Apple Calendar, Calendly, and most major shop management systems like Shop-Ware, Mitchell 1, and Tekmetric.

Do you offer a money-back guarantee?

We offer month-to-month service with no long-term contracts. If you're not happy, simply cancel with 30 days notice. No questions asked.
