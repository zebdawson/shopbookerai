About ShopBooker AI

We're on a mission to help every auto repair shop capture every customer opportunity

Our Story

ShopBooker AI was born from a simple observation: hardworking shop owners were losing thousands of dollars every month simply because they couldn't answer every phone call.

Our founder, Zeb, watched his friend run a successful 4-bay auto repair shop. Business was booming, but there was one problem - phones rang constantly while the team was busy working on cars. Every unanswered call meant lost revenue.

The solution seemed obvious: hire a receptionist. But the cost of a full-time employee with benefits would eat into already thin margins. And even with a receptionist, what about after hours? Nights? Weekends? Holidays?

That's when we realized AI could solve this problem perfectly. An AI assistant could answer every call, book every appointment, handle every inquiry - 24/7/365, for a fraction of the cost of a human receptionist.

ShopBooker AI was built specifically for auto repair shops by people who understand the industry.
