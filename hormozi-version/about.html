The Problem That Started It All

"I was elbows-deep in a transmission rebuild when my phone rang for the fifth time that hour. I couldn't answer - my hands were covered in grease. By the time I cleaned up and called back, they'd already gone to the shop down the street."

That single missed call cost me $2,400 in revenue.

After running my shop for 12 years, I realized this was happening dozens of times per week. I tried hiring a receptionist, but she called in sick. I tried an answering service, but they couldn't book appointments or answer technical questions.

So I partnered with AI engineers to build exactly what I needed: an assistant who understands automotive repair, never takes a day off, and books appointments 24/7.

That's how ShopBooker AI was born.
