Real-World Scenarios

See how ShopBooker AI handles different types of calls

After Hours Call

🕒 9:00 PM - Shop is closed

Customer: "Hi, my check engine light just came on. Can I bring it in tomorrow?"

AI: "Of course! Let me check our availability for tomorrow. Do you prefer morning or afternoon?"

Result: Appointment booked for 10 AM the next day, confirmation sent.

Captured what would have been a lost opportunity

Busy Time Call

🔧 2:00 PM - You're under a car

Customer: "I need an oil change this week. What do you have available?"

AI: "I can get you in Thursday at 2 PM or Friday at 11 AM. Which works better?"

Result: Appointment booked while you kept working. No interruption.

You stayed productive, customer got booked

Rescheduling Request

📞 Customer needs to change appointment

Customer: "I have an appointment tomorrow at 10, but something came up. Can I move it?"

AI: "No problem! I can reschedule you for next Tuesday at 10 AM or Wednesday at 2 PM."

Result: Appointment moved, calendar updated, confirmation sent.

Handled without bothering you

General Inquiry

❓ Customer has questions

Customer: "Do you do transmission work? How much does a fluid change cost?"

AI: "Yes, we do! A transmission fluid change typically runs $150-200. Would you like to schedule one?"

Result: Question answered, appointment booked if interested.

Turned inquiry into booking
